# Univer Surface Breadcrumb Route

When a bound Univer session is selected, the right-side Univer Surface is anchored to the session's Primary Univerfile, not to the chat session itself. Its header renders a breadcrumb, `<Univerfile> / <Unit> / <Worktree>`, because unit and worktree selection are view-route choices under the file; they must not mutate the session's Primary Univerfile binding or Session Univer Worktree ownership.
